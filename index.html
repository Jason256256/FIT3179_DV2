Section 1: Enrolment Rates in Australia

This section explores early childhood education uptake across various community remoteness levels, gender divergence via difference mapping, and state-level geographic disparities.

Key Insights
  • Remoteness Gap: Participation drops sharply from ~90-95% in Major Cities down to ~58-68% in Very Remote regions.
  • Gender Trend: Female enrolment consistently leads male participation by ~1.3% nationwide across 2016-2026.
  • State Disparity: The ACT and southeastern states lead national rates (>90%), while the Northern Territory lags at ~80%.

Section 2: Attendance Hours in Australia

This section examines average weekly hours spent in early childhood education across community remoteness levels, genders, and regional jurisdictions.

Key Insights
  • Remoteness Gap: Children in Major Cities attend ~6.4 hours/weekday compared to ~4.1 hours in Very Remote areas.
  • Gender Trend: Females consistently have longer attendence times, albeit slightly than males and higher remoteness also correlates to shorter attendance time
  • Regional Leaders: ACT consistently records the highest average weekday participation durations nationwide, followed by Victoria.
